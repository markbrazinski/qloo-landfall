Sparkling cold-brewed tea

Tidewell is sparkling cold-brewed tea, brewed slowly in Portland and canned while it is still bright. No syrups, no sweeteners, just tea, a little fruit and bubbles.

We started on a single stall at a neighbourhood market and now you can find us in shops across Portland and Seattle. Pick up a can after your Saturday run or order a mixed case from our shop.

Four flavours, all in 12oz cans: Black Tea and Lemon, Green Tea and Yuzu, Hibiscus and Berry, and Oolong and Peach.
