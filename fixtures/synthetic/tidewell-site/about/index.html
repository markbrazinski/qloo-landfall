Our story

Tidewell began in 2021 in a shared kitchen in Southeast Portland. We wanted something to drink after long runs that tasted like real tea and was not loaded with sugar.

We brew in small batches and work with neighbourhood shops that know their customers by name. We would rather be on the counter of a corner grocer than at the end of a supermarket aisle.

We are a team of five, and we still deliver to many of our stockists ourselves.
