Shipping and questions

Where do you ship? We ship within the US only. We do not ship internationally yet.

How long does delivery take? Orders leave our Portland warehouse within two working days and usually arrive within a week.

Is there caffeine? Yes, every flavour contains caffeine from the tea, between 30 and 45mg per can.
